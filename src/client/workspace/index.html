WorldKinetics Workspace / revision study

A guide to the prototype

From a useful change to a checked, editable design

Describe a useful part change, confirm its dimensions, then inspect the revised design and its checks before accepting it.

Inspect the workspace
  1. Start with a reference. Bring known measurements.
  2. Confirm, then request. Explicitly confirm requirements before requesting a change.
  3. Follow actual events. Activity reports progress and failures.
  4. Inspect the revised geometry. Read checks tied to that exact revision.
  5. Review, then explicitly accept. Completion never accepts a candidate.
  6. Download exact accepted files. They stay tied to the accepted revision, even while you inspect another.

Recorded result summary

These retained numeric API/CAD results describe a recorded run, not a fresh run or video.

A 30 mm length yielded 2 mm of end material and was rejected against the unchanged 5 mm minimum. A separately confirmed 36 mm requirement, through explicit API confirmation, yielded 5 mm of end material; 7 checks passed.

The result satisfies the new 36 mm requirement, not the original 30 mm request. Final dimensions: length 36 mm, width 35 mm, thickness 5 mm. Two 6 mm diameter through holes retain a 20 mm center pitch.

Astra planned numeric changes; template Python generated the plate geometry. Digital checks do not establish physical fit or manufacturing readiness.
