          What WorldKinetics checks
          
      
          Eight checks for the starting handle. A ninth checks the grip-and-thumb-rest refinement. These are the rules, not a claim that your current design passed.
          
      
            
        
          1. One valid solidThe geometry forms one connected, valid shape.
            2. 
        
          2. Mounting interfacePreserve the mounting pads and 96 mm spacing.
            3. 
        
          3. Overall sizeStay within the allowed envelope and 140 mm maximum length.
            4. 
        
          4. Finger clearanceKeep at least 25 mm of empty finger space.
            5. 
        
          5. Grip continuityKeep the grip connected across the required sections.
            6. 
        
          6. STEP exportReopen the CAD file and compare it with the checked geometry.
            7. 
        
          7. STL exportCheck that the mesh is connected, watertight and matches the solid within tolerance.
            8. 
        
          8. Editable sourceRerun the delivered source and compare the recreated geometry.
            9. 
        
          9. Requested refinementVerify the broader grip and thumb rest while preserving the accepted starting design.
            10. 
      
          
      
          Astra cannot lower these thresholds or approve the result. Live pass/fail results appear under Design checks for the displayed revision. These checks do not certify physical fit or strength.
          
    
          
    
          
      
          Get it made
          
      
          Your next steps after downloading. Supplier guidance is available before approval; CAD downloads still require an approved version.
          
      
            
        
          1. Download the complete package. In the recorded demo, select After and Complete approved package · ZIP. In live mode, approve your design, then choose Download handoff package.
            2. 
        
          2. Unzip and upload the CAD. Use candidate.step for a CAD quote or preview.stl for a 3D-print quote. Units are millimeters. Share PROTOTYPE-BRIEF.txt and RFQ.txt with the supplier.
            3. 
        
          3. Get a quote. Confirm material, quantity, finish and delivery location. Compare part cost, setup fees, shipping and taxes, plus production time and transit time separately.
            4. 
        
          4. Order a prototype on the supplier’s site. Check its fit before ordering more. Mounting hardware is unspecified; geometry checks do not establish physical strength or manufacturing readiness.

          What’s inside the complete ZIP
        
          
          
          candidate.step
          Editable solid CAD. Send to a supplier or open in a compatible CAD application.
          
          
          preview.stl
          Triangle mesh. Open in a slicer or upload for a print quote, using millimeters.
          
          
          source.py and editable.py
          Python CAD source for editing and recreating the model. Requires the recorded build123d environment and input files; not printer instructions.
          
          
          PROTOTYPE-BRIEF.txt
          Read first: approved design, requirements and prototype limitations.
          
          
          RFQ.txt and RFQ.json
          The quote request and preferences for this approved revision. RFQ.txt can be copied into a supplier inquiry.
          
          
          package.json
          File inventory and hashes for checking package contents.
          
          
          evidence/
          acceptance.json, manifest.json, requirements.json, checks.json and reference.json. Refinements also include initial-acceptance.json and initial-manifest.json.
          
          
          input/
          reference.step and datums.json retain the mounting reference. A refinement also includes baseline.step, its approved starting geometry.
          
        
          
        
          No printer-ready G-code or dimensioned manufacturing drawing is included. Keep all files together for recreating the CAD.
